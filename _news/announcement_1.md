I am pleased to announce that I have joined the the [IUT of Montreuil](https://www.univ-paris8.fr/IUT-de-Montreuil) - [University of Paris 8](https://www.univ-paris8.fr/) as an Assistant Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science. I am affiliated with the [Laboratoire d’Intelligence Artificielle et Sémantique des Données (LIASD)](https://www.univ-paris8.fr/UR-Laboratoire-d-Intelligence-Artificielle-et-Semantique-des-Donnees-LIASD) and a member of the _Espaces Intelligents de Données (EID)_ research team.

---

My research lies at the intersection of the Semantic Web and Artificial Intelligence, with a particular focus on knowledge representation and reasoning, knowledge graphs, semantic interoperability, and large language models. My work aims to advance neuro-symbolic approaches that combine symbolic methods with data-driven techniques to improve robustness, explainability, and trustworthiness in AI systems.

---

At Paris 8, I contribute to research activities within LIASD and teach computer science courses at the IUT of Montreuil, covering topics related to programming, software engineering, data, and artificial intelligence. I look forward to developing new collaborations within LIASD, the University of Paris 8, and the broader research community.

---

I am grateful to my colleagues, collaborators, and mentors for their support throughout this journey and look forward to this new chapter.
