ORIGAMI

Online Tool for the Identification and Prioritization of Asbestos-Containing Materials

National Research Projects

November 2018 - April 2022

PRDA (Plan de Recherche et de Développement Amiante), initiated by the French Ministry of Housing

Project website: https://www.habitat-reuni.fr/habitat-reuni-et-le-cstb-signent-une-convention-sur-le-partage-de-donnees-amiante

During my doctoral research activities (Mecharnia, 2022), I contributed to the ORIGAMI project, conducted in the context of the PRDA, in which the CSTB (Centre Scientifique et Technique du Bâtiment) was tasked with developing an online decision-support tool for identifying building materials potentially containing asbestos.

The objective of the project was to design a system assisting operators in asbestos inspection by guiding the identification process and prioritizing buildings for diagnostic assessments. My work (Mecharnia et al., 2019; Mecharnia et al., 2019; Mecharnia et al., 2021; Mecharnia et al., 2021) focused on the formalization of domain knowledge and the development of decision-support mechanisms to improve the reliability, efficiency, and consistency of asbestos risk assessment in buildings.
