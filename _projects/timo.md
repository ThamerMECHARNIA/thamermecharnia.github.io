### Personal Project

**September 2016 - April 2017**

**Microsoft Imagine Cup Competition**

Demonstration video: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dFKbWlNp5AE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dFKbWlNp5AE)

I designed and developed the 3D video game **TIMO** as part of the **Microsoft Imagine Cup** competition. The project focused on creating an immersive interactive experience, combining gameplay mechanics, narrative elements, and 3D environments. This work involved full-cycle game development, from concept and prototyping to implementation and demonstration.
