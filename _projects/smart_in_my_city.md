### Personal Project

**December 2016 - May 2017**

**UNICEF Innovation Lab**

Demonstration video: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ups8U0REEMA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ups8U0REEMA)

I co-developed the 3D serious game **Smart In My City** in the context of **UNICEF Innovation Lab** competition in [ESI (_Ecole Nationale Supérieure d'Informatique (Ex. INI)_)](https://www.esi.dz/). The project aimed to raise awareness among young audiences about urban challenges and civic engagement through interactive gameplay. My contributions included game design, 3D development, and the integration of interactive mechanics to support educational objectives.
