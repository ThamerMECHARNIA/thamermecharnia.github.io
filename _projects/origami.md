### National Research Projects

**November 2018 - April 2022**

**PRDA (Plan de Recherche et de Développement Amiante), initiated by the French Ministry of Housing**

Project website: [https://www.habitat-reuni.fr/habitat-reuni-et-le-cstb-signent-une-convention-sur-le-partage-de-donnees-amiante](https://www.habitat-reuni.fr/habitat-reuni-et-le-cstb-signent-une-convention-sur-le-partage-de-donnees-amiante)

During my doctoral research activities {% cite DBLP:phd/hal/Mecharnia22 %}, I contributed to the ORIGAMI project, conducted in the context of the PRDA, in which the [CSTB](https://www.cstb.fr/) (_Centre Scientifique et Technique du Bâtiment_) was tasked with developing an online decision-support tool for identifying building materials potentially containing asbestos.

The objective of the project was to design a system assisting operators in asbestos inspection by guiding the identification process and prioritizing buildings for diagnostic assessments. My work {% cite DBLP:conf/kcap/MecharniaKPH19 DBLP:conf/f-ic/MecharniaPK019 DBLP:conf/iccs/MecharniaPRHK21 DBLP:conf/f-ic/MecharniaK0PR21 %} focused on the formalization of domain knowledge and the development of decision-support mechanisms to improve the reliability, efficiency, and consistency of asbestos risk assessment in buildings.
