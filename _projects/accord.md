### European Research Project

**November 2022 - August 2023**

**Horizon Europe project (HORIZON-CL4-2021-TWIN-TRANSITION-01)**

Project website: [https://accordproject.eu/](https://accordproject.eu/)

During my postdoctoral research at [École des Mines de Saint-Étienne (EMSE)](https://www.mines-stetienne.fr/), I contributed to the ACCORD European project {% cite DBLP:data/10/HettiarachchiDGPBBJHJMP23 %}, which aims to digitize and automate authorization and regulatory compliance processes in the construction domain. The project leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) of regulatory texts and integrates heterogeneous data sources, including Building Information Modeling (BIM), CityGML, and domain-specific data dictionaries.

My work focused on **semantic modeling and semantic interoperability** {% cite mecharnia2024etude mecharnia2023accord %}, particularly on aligning regulatory knowledge with building and urban data models, in order to support automated compliance checking, improve decision support, and enhance the reliability and efficiency of design and construction workflows.
