I am an Assistant Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science at the University of Paris 8, and a member of the [LIASD](https://www.univ-paris8.fr/UR-Laboratoire-d-Intelligence-Artificielle-et-Semantique-des-Donnees-LIASD) (Laboratoire d'Intelligence Artificielle et Sémantique des Données), within the EID (Espaces Intelligents de Données) team. My research lies at the intersection of the Semantic Web and Artificial Intelligence (AI), with a particular emphasis on knowledge representation and reasoning, symbolic AI, and large language models (LLMs). I work on the integration of symbolic AI methods with data-driven approaches - especially LLMs - to address challenges such as natural language question answering over knowledge graphs and SPARQL query generation and correction. In parallel, I develop hybrid neuro-symbolic approaches aimed at improving the robustness, explainability, and trustworthiness of AI systems, with applications ranging from scientific data analysis to decentralized and blockchain-based data ecosystems.

I also teach computer science courses at the [IUT of Montreuil](https://www.univ-paris8.fr/IUT-de-Montreuil).

### Research Interests

- **Symbolic Artificial Intelligence:** rule-based learning, association rule mining, and case-based reasoning.
- **Knowledge Graphs & Data Graphs:** modeling, querying, and reasoning over structured and semi-structured data.
- **Knowledge Representation & Automated Reasoning:** logic-based formalisms, inference mechanisms, and explainability.
- **Semantic Web & Semantic Interoperability:** ontology engineering and data integration.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** neuro-symbolic integration, natural language question answering, and SPARQL query generation and correction.
- **Blockchain & Decentralized Data Ecosystems:** semantic modeling and data traceability.
